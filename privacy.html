Who we are

BuyerWise provides flat-fee home buyer's agent representation in Washington State (WA License #25007529), operating through Pellego. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit the consultation booking form on this site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number

We may also collect standard analytics and advertising data (such as pages viewed and referral source) through tools like Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel, if and when those are enabled on this site.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Contact you about booking and completing a free consultation
  • Follow up regarding buyer representation services
  • Improve this website and our advertising based on aggregate usage trends

We do not sell your personal information.

How your information is shared

Form submissions are processed through Formspree, our third-party form handling provider. If you schedule a consultation, appointment details are processed through Calendly. If analytics or advertising pixels (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel) are active on this site, those providers may also receive standard usage data as described in their own privacy policies.

Your choices
