1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit our consultation booking form or schedule an appointment, we collect the personal information you provide, which may include your full name, email address, and telephone number. If you contact us directly by phone or email, we collect the information you choose to share in that communication.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, and the date and time of your visit. This information is collected through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies as described in Section 3 below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To schedule, confirm, and prepare for consultations and appointments
  • To respond to inquiries and provide buyer representation services
  • To communicate with you about your home search and our services
  • To measure and improve the performance of this Site and our advertising, including campaigns run through Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta") and other advertising partners
  • To maintain records as required by Washington real estate licensing law
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies, Pixels & Similar Technologies

This Site may use cookies and similar tracking technologies, including the Meta Pixel and/or Meta Conversions API and Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the Site and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, including ads served on Facebook and Instagram.

These tools may collect information about your browsing activity on this Site and share it with Meta and other advertising partners for purposes such as ad targeting and measurement. Meta's collection and use of this information is governed independently by Meta's Data Policy, available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Your choices: You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Meta's Ad Preferences, Google's Ad Settings, or through the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may affect Site functionality.

4. How We Share Your Information

We disclose personal information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Formspree to process form submissions and Calendly to schedule consultations. These providers process information on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using it for their own purposes.
  • Advertising partners: Meta and Google may receive limited technical and usage data through pixels or conversion tracking, as described in Section 3.
  • Brokerage affiliation: As a licensed real estate professional operating under Pellego, relevant client information may be shared with our affiliated brokerage as necessary to provide representation services.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BuyerWise, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers: Information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

5. Telephone & Text Message Consent

By submitting your phone number through our consultation form, you consent to receive calls and text messages from BuyerWise related to your inquiry and our services, which may be sent using automated technology. Consent to receive such communications is not a condition of receiving services. Message and data rates may apply. You may withdraw consent at any time by replying "STOP" to a text message or by contacting us using the information in Section 12.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including to provide services, maintain business and licensing records as required under Washington real estate law, and comply with our legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or de-identify it.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal recordkeeping obligations
  • Opt out of future marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Washington State Disclosures

BuyerWise is based in and provides services within Washington State. As of the date of this Policy, Washington has not enacted a comprehensive consumer data privacy statute of general applicability to a business of this size and nature. Nonetheless, we voluntarily commit to the practices described throughout this Policy, including not selling personal information.

In the event of a breach of unencrypted personal information that meets the threshold under Washington's data breach notification law (RCW 19.255), we will notify affected individuals and, where required, the Washington State Attorney General, in accordance with that statute.

BuyerWise operates as a licensed real estate professional under Pellego, WA Real Estate License #25007529, regulated by the Washington State Department of Licensing. This Privacy Policy is separate from, and supplements, any agency disclosures provided to you under Washington real estate law.
