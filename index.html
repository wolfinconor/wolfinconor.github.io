How seller contributions work

  1. You agree to one fee with us: $5,000. That number is set when you sign your buyer brokerage agreement. It's the only compensation you're negotiating with us, and it doesn't change with the price of the home.
  2. The seller may contribute. Your offer can ask the seller to pay toward buyer-broker compensation. Whatever the seller agrees to pay is effectively an incentive to you: it covers our fee first.
  3. Any surplus comes back to you. Under NWMLS Form 41C, any amount the seller contributes beyond our $5,000 fee is credited to you, as your lender allows. If the seller contributes less than $5,000, or nothing, you pay the difference. Either way, you never pay us more than $5,000.

Because the actual benefit depends on what the seller contributes on each deal, the figures above are an illustration, not a guarantee.
